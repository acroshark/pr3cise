Construction cost estimating backed by decades of building experience

Pr3cise is a remote construction cost estimating consultancy: you send your quantity takeoff and we price it line by line. Backed by decades of building construction experience, every estimate is prepared directly by a technical architect for general contractors, subcontractors, developers and architects, primarily in the State of Florida, with clients across Europe and around the world. No border limits.

Who prepares a Pr3cise estimate, and how we operate

Every Pr3cise estimate is prepared directly by a technical architect (arquitecto técnico / quantity surveyor) with decades of experience in building construction. Pr3cise is operated remotely by Clear Ascend OÜ, registered in Estonia (Reg. Code 17448615); we do not maintain physical branches or premises in the United States or other countries, and no site visits are involved. Pr3cise never bids construction work itself, so the takeoffs, vendor quotes and commercial rates a client shares are treated strictly confidentially and are never in competition with the consultancy pricing them.

Pr3cise prices work with current regional cost data cross-checked against supplier quotes, and organizes every deliverable the way your team already works — CSI MasterFormat divisions, a bill of quantities, BC3, or your own spreadsheet template when you have one.

Our turnaround is straightforward arithmetic. Our working day starts seven hours ahead of US Eastern Time for most of the year (six hours during brief daylight-saving transition weeks) and one hour ahead of Central European Time, so a takeoff sent at the close of your business day is priced overnight while your office is closed. For a contractor in Florida that is a full extra day on every bid; for one in Europe it means a priced estimate ready in the morning.

Construction literacy, not just software

Knowing how a building goes together is how you spot which line items are missing from a takeoff. The omissions that erode margins are rarely exotic — they are the assemblies nobody thought to list.

Local building codes priced properly

CSI MasterFormat structure and regional cost data for the market where the project is built — accounting for Florida Building Code, HVHZ and NOA cost drivers on a Miami-Dade job, the Eurocodes and their national annexes across Europe, and the local code wherever else you build.

A number you can defend

An estimate is only useful if it holds up when someone challenges it. Every Pr3cise estimate ships with its unit-price breakdowns, written assumptions and exclusions attached so you can show your work.

Where we work

One remote consultancy with no border limits: primarily the State of Florida, with clients across Europe and around the world. Clear Ascend OÜ is registered in Estonia and operates without physical branches abroad: takeoffs arrive and estimates ship digitally, while the deliverable format and code basis adapt to the country where the project is built. We work in English and Spanish.

  • State of Florida (primary focus) — CSI MasterFormat line items with regional cost data. Across Miami-Dade and South Florida we price Florida Building Code, High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) and Notice of Acceptance (NOA) assembly requirements from day one.
  • Across Europe — priced bills of quantities and BC3 deliverables in the structure your tender documents require, priced against the Eurocodes and the national code where you build.
  • Worldwide, no border limits — the structure your tender requires (CSI MasterFormat, BoQ or your own template), metric or imperial, in your currency.
