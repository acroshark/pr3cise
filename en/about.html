Construction cost estimating backed by decades of building experience

Pr3cise is a remote construction cost estimating and quantity takeoff consultancy. Backed by decades of building construction experience, every estimate is prepared directly by a technical architect for general contractors, subcontractors, developers and architects in the United States, the United Kingdom, Spain, the Gulf and across Europe.

Who prepares a Pr3cise estimate, and how we operate

Every Pr3cise estimate is prepared directly by a technical architect (arquitecto técnico / quantity surveyor) with decades of experience in building construction. Pr3cise is operated remotely by Clear Ascend OÜ, registered in Estonia (Reg. Code 17448615); we do not maintain physical branches or premises in the United States or other countries, and no site visits are involved. Pr3cise never bids construction work itself, so the drawings, vendor quotes and commercial rates a client shares are treated strictly confidentially and are never in competition with the consultancy measuring them.

Pr3cise prices work with current regional cost data cross-checked against supplier quotes, and organizes every deliverable the way your team already works — CSI MasterFormat divisions for North American projects, a bill of quantities for tenders that call for one, BC3 in Spain, or your own spreadsheet template when you have one.

Our turnaround is straightforward arithmetic. Our working day starts seven hours ahead of US Eastern Time for most of the year (six hours during brief daylight-saving transition weeks) and one hour ahead of Madrid, so drawings sent at the close of your business day are measured overnight while your office is closed. For a contractor in the Americas that is a full extra day on every bid; for one in Europe it means a completed takeoff ready in the morning.

Construction literacy, not just software

Knowing how a building goes together is how you spot which line items are missing from a drawing set. The omissions that erode margins are rarely exotic — they are the assemblies nobody thought to quantify.

Local building codes priced properly

CSI MasterFormat structure and regional cost data for the market where the project is built — accounting for Florida Building Code, HVHZ and NOA cost drivers on a Miami-Dade job, and CTE or Eurocode requirements in Spain and Europe.

A number you can defend

An estimate is only useful if it holds up when someone challenges it. Every Pr3cise estimate ships with colour-coded takeoff markups, written assumptions and exclusions attached so you can show your work.

Where we work

One remote consultancy serving international markets. Clear Ascend OÜ is registered in Estonia and operates without physical branches abroad: drawings arrive and estimates ship digitally, while the deliverable format and code basis adapt to the country where the project is built. We work in English and Spanish.

  • United States — CSI MasterFormat line items with regional cost data. Miami-Dade and South Florida is our priority US market, pricing Florida Building Code, High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) and Notice of Acceptance (NOA) assembly requirements from day one.
  • United Kingdom and Ireland — bills of quantities and pre-tender cost plans in the structure your tender documents require.
  • Spain and Spanish-speaking markets — mediciones y presupuestos in Spanish, delivered in BC3 as well as Excel and PDF, priced against CTE, REBT and RSCIEI.
  • United Arab Emirates, the Gulf and Europe — BoQ-based tender pricing for Gulf projects and Eurocodes with their national annexes across the EU.
