Bidding blind is how contractors lose money twice

Roughly 70% of construction projects run over their original budget. Bid too high and you never get the job. Bid too low and you win the job that eats your margin for the next eighteen months. Both failures come from the same place: a takeoff that missed something.

An estimate is not paperwork. It is the document that decides whether the project makes you money, and it deserves more than the last two nights before bid day.

How we build your estimate

Four steps, the same on every job, so you always know what you are getting and when.

01

Scope review

We read the drawings, specs and addenda before touching a number, and we come back to you with the gaps, conflicts and exclusions we find. That conversation alone catches most of the money.

02

Quantity takeoff

Every line item measured and organized by CSI MasterFormat division — materials, labor and equipment. Color-coded markups ship with the estimate so you can audit any quantity in seconds.

03

Pricing & value engineering

Current regional material and labor pricing, cross-checked against supplier quotes. Where a spec is driving cost without driving value, we flag the alternate and price both.

04

Delivery

Excel and PDF, in your template if you have one, with overhead, profit and waste factors broken out separately so your margin is visible and defensible in a bid review.
