Frequently asked questions

Pricing, turnaround and formats, before you send your drawings.

A construction estimating service is an outsourced preconstruction service that measures your drawings and prices the work, so you receive a line-item estimate you can bid from. Pr3cise handles both halves of it, the quantity takeoff and the cost estimate: materials, labor, equipment, overhead and profit, broken out by CSI MasterFormat division. Outside North America the same work is usually bought as quantity surveying: a bill of quantities (BoQ) or pre-tender estimate — we deliver in that format too.

A full-time estimator is a fixed cost whether or not you are bidding. Outsourcing turns that into a variable cost per bid, removes the errors that creep in when your team is overloaded, and lets you chase more bids in the same week without adding headcount.

Drawings in PDF or DWG and the project specifications are enough. If you already have an internal template, an addendum set or a scope letter, send those too — we will match your format.

Pr3cise delivers in 24 to 72 hours from receipt of a complete document set, depending on scope and trade. The delivery date and the fixed fee are confirmed in writing before work starts.

Both, plus developers and architects. General contractors typically come to us for full multi-trade budgets; subcontractors for single-trade takeoffs and bid-day or tender pricing.

Excel and PDF as standard, with a color-coded markup set so you can see exactly where every quantity came from. We can also deliver into your own bid template.

Entirely by file, and the time difference works in your favor. Our working day starts about seven hours ahead of Florida, so drawings you send at the end of your day are measured during your night and you gain a day on every bid.

Mostly in the State of Florida, with clients across Europe and the rest of the world. No border limits: everything is exchanged digitally, in imperial or metric units, in your currency and to the code that governs your project, structured as CSI MasterFormat, a bill of quantities or BC3.
