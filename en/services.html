Construction estimating services from your quantity takeoff

Outsourced estimating prepared directly by a technical architect — line-item cost estimating and tender pricing from your quantity takeoff — by trade or full building package, wherever you build. Fixed project fee and delivery date agreed in writing.

Pr3cise provides construction estimating services to general contractors, subcontractors, developers and architects. You send your quantity takeoff; a technical architect with decades of building construction experience prices every line and returns a line-item estimate or priced bill of quantities in Excel and PDF, normally within 24 to 72 hours of a complete takeoff. We do not measure from drawings. The fee is fixed per project and agreed in writing before any work starts. Pr3cise is a remote consultancy — Clear Ascend OÜ, registered in Estonia — with no premises in the United States and no site visits: every job travels digitally as a file, in English or Spanish.

Pricing of your quantity takeoff

Your takeoff is the foundation every other number rests on: we keep your line items and quantities, organized by CSI division or your own format, and price each one. How we price your takeoff, in detail.

Cost estimating

Your takeoff quantities priced by a cost estimator against current regional material and labor rates, including equipment, overhead, profit and waste factors — not a national average applied to your job.

Conceptual and budget estimating

Early-stage numbers for feasibility and lender packages, priced from your preliminary quantities and updated as the takeoff firms up.

MEP estimating

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing priced as a package or by trade. Our electrical estimating services run from the service entrance to the devices; our plumbing and piping estimating services cover domestic water, waste, storm, gas and process pipe priced by diameter and material — plus fire protection and low-voltage.

Structural and civil

Concrete, masonry, structural steel, earthwork and sitework, with formwork and rebar broken out rather than buried in an allowance.

Finishes and specialty trades

Drywall, framing, roofing, flooring, painting, glazing and millwork — quantified to the assembly, not estimated by floor area.

What is in every estimate Pr3cise delivers

  • Line-item breakdown by CSI MasterFormat division — or the classification your market works with — so it drops straight into your bid form or accounting system.
  • Material, labor and equipment separated on every line, with labor hours and crew rates visible rather than rolled into a lump sum.
  • Waste factors priced per material — tile, aggregate and steel do not waste at the same rate, and pretending they do costs money on procurement.
  • Overhead and profit broken out as their own lines, adjustable to your numbers, so your margin survives a bid review.
  • Every price next to its line of your takeoff, so any line can be audited in seconds.
  • A written scope and exclusions list — what we included, what we did not, and what the takeoff left ambiguous.
  • One revision round included, for addenda, spec changes or price updates.

Where we work

Pr3cise is a remote estimating consultancy — Clear Ascend OÜ, registered in Estonia — with no physical branch you need to drive to. This is how an outsourced estimating consultancy works alongside your own office. Every job travels as a file: you send your quantity takeoff, a technical architect prices it line by line, and the estimate comes back in the format and against the building code of the market the project is actually built in. We work in English and Spanish.

  • State of Florida (primary focus) — line items by CSI MasterFormat division, priced with regional material and labor data. Across Miami-Dade and South Florida, the Florida Building Code, High-Velocity Hurricane Zone assemblies and Notice of Acceptance product approvals are priced in from the start rather than added later.
  • Across Europe — priced bills of quantities and BC3 deliverables in the structure the tender documents ask for, priced against the Eurocodes and their national annexes: ready to submit rather than ready to retype.
  • Worldwide, no border limits — the structure your tender requires (CSI MasterFormat, BoQ or your own template), metric or imperial, in your currency.
