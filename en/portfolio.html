Sample estimates and what makes them different

We deliver a document you can bid from, buy from and defend: every line item priced, and every quantity traceable back to the drawing it came from.

Waste factors priced per material

Tile, aggregate and structural steel do not waste at the same rate. We price each one to its real coefficient instead of applying a blanket 10% and hoping. That is the difference between a procurement budget and a guess.

Overhead and profit broken out

General conditions, overhead and profit appear as their own line items, adjustable to your numbers. When an owner or a bid reviewer asks what your markup is, you have an answer.

Current regional pricing

Material and labor priced with cost data refreshed monthly and cross-checked against live supplier quotes for the market the project is actually in.

Auditable takeoff markups

Every quantity ties back to a color-coded markup on the drawing set. If a number is questioned, you can show exactly where it came from without redoing the work.
